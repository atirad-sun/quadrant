Shortcuts & tips

Quick-add codes: type them anywhere in the task:
!1 Do First · !2 Schedule · !3 Delegate · !4 Eliminate
#tag adds a category  ·  @today @tmr @fri @3d @2w @2026-10-15 sets the deadline
🔁 @daily @weekdays @weekly @monthly @every-mon @every-2w repeats the task: finish it and the next one appears

Keys: N add a task · / search · V switch view · Esc close / cancel drag
On a focused card (Tab or an arrow key to start): ←↑→↓ move between cards · 1–4 send to Do First…Eliminate · 0 back to Inbox · Shift+↑↓ reorder · Enter open
Mouse: drag cards between boxes, or drop them on a colored tile at the top · click a card to edit · click a category chip to filter · click a day in the calendar to see what's due

💾 Tasks are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Use ⋯ → Export backup now and then, and to move your tasks to another device.
